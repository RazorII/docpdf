# Privacy

docpdf and its Claude plugin collect no data.

- **Your documents stay on your machine.** docpdf converts them there, with LibreOffice. The documents, the PDFs and the check results never leave it.
- **docpdf sends nothing anywhere.** No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reports, no account.
- **The only network traffic is installing it.** The plugin's skill downloads docpdf from [GitHub](https://github.com/RazorII/docpdf) and its one dependency, pypdf, from [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/pypdf/). If LibreOffice is missing and you agree to install it, your system's package manager downloads it. Those services' own privacy policies cover those downloads.
- **Claude itself is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.** When you use docpdf through Claude, what Claude does with your conversation, including any document you share in it, falls under [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy), not this one.

Questions go to [Discussions](https://github.com/RazorII/docpdf/discussions/categories/q-a).
